# Invoicerr trademark and logo policy

Invoicerr the software is free: it is published under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, and
that licence gives you the right to use, study, change and redistribute the code.

The **name "Invoicerr" and the logos in this repository are not covered by that licence.** They are
how people tell our work from someone else's, and a licence that let anyone put our name on their
software would destroy exactly that. This page says what you may do with them without asking us.

## What you may do, no permission needed

- **Write about Invoicerr.** Reviews, tutorials, comparisons, criticism, blog posts, books, talks,
  videos, courses. Use the name as much as the subject requires. You do not need our approval, and we
  do not want to see it in advance.
- **Link to us**, and use the logo as the link's image.
- **Say that your thing works with Invoicerr.** "X for Invoicerr", "Invoicerr plugin", "imports from
  Invoicerr", "hosting for Invoicerr" are all fine, as long as the sentence makes clear that Invoicerr
  is ours and the other thing is yours.
- **Display the logo on an Invoicerr instance you run**, including one you host for other people, as
  long as it is Invoicerr and you have not modified it in a way the next section rules out.
- **Redistribute the software unmodified**, with its name and logo in place.
- Use the assets in this repository as they are: resized, and placed on either a light or a dark
  ground using the variants provided.

## What you may not do without asking us

- **Name a derived product after us.** If you modify Invoicerr and redistribute it, the AGPL says you
  may; this policy says you have to give it a different name and a different logo. You are welcome to
  state factually that it is based on Invoicerr.
- **Imply that we endorse, sponsor, certify, partner with or approve** you, your product, your
  company, your fork or your service.
- **Use the name or logo in your own company name, product name, domain name, app-store listing,
  social-media handle or logo**, or in anything a reasonable person could mistake for an official
  Invoicerr channel.
- **Alter the logo**: do not recolour it outside the variants here, stretch it, rotate it, add effects,
  redraw it, or set it inside another mark. The ink is `#0f1f2a` and the azure is `#0099cb`; the white
  version exists for dark grounds so you never need to invent one.
- **Sell merchandise** whose value comes from carrying our name or mark.

## If your case is not here

Ask: open an issue on this repository, or write to the address on
[invoicerr.app](https://invoicerr.app). We would rather say yes to something reasonable than have you
guess.

## Reporting misuse

If you find someone passing their software off as Invoicerr, or claiming an affiliation that does not
exist, tell us through the same channels.
